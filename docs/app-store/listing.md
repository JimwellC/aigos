## Description (≤4000) — ~2,350 chars
```
Magkano ang gas papuntang Baguio? Now you'll know — before you even start the engine.

aigos is the fuel-trip calculator built for Filipino roads. Gas prices keep climbing, traffic on EDSA eats your tank, and every probinsya drive turns into a guessing game. aigos turns that guess into a real peso figure you can trust.

Pick your vehicle, enter where you're headed, and see exactly what the trip will cost — down to the litre and the peso.

WHY DRIVERS LOVE IT
• Works 100% offline. No signal on the NLEX? No problem. Everything runs on your iPhone — no internet needed, ever.
• No login, no account, no cloud. Open it and go. Your data never leaves your device.
• One-time purchase. No subscriptions, no ads, no tracking. Buy it once, it's yours.

NOT JUST A NUMBER — REAL ACCURACY
Most apps just multiply distance by a guess. aigos uses real physics: your speed, aircon, road type (highway, city, mountain, mixed), traffic (from light to standstill), and how loaded the car is. Rush-hour city crawling burns far more fuel than an open highway — and aigos knows the difference. You see the full breakdown, not a mystery total.

TALK TO AIGOS
Just ask in plain English: "How much to drive Manila to Batangas?" or "Pampanga to Manila with heavy traffic." aigos understands and answers on the spot — entirely on-device, still no internet needed.

KNOWS YOUR CAR
Not sure of your car's mileage? Ask aigos. It has a built-in database of the top Philippine vehicles — Vios, Innova, Fortuner, Mirage, Wigo, City, Xpander and more, plus popular motorcycles like the Mio, NMAX, and Click. Get realistic km/L and use it in one tap.

BUILT FOR THE PESO
• PH fuel grades: Unleaded 91, Premium 95 & 97, Diesel, Premium Diesel, E-Gasoline
• Edit any price to match your favorite station
• See your route on a map with the distance
• Save regular routes for one-tap recalculation
• Track your fuel spending month over month
• aigos learns your driving style the more you use it

FOR EVERY FILIPINO DRIVER
Budgeting the family beach trip, planning the daily commute, or running a business on wheels — aigos helps you drive smarter and spend less.

Know before you go. Download aigos.

Private. Offline. Sakto para sa Pinoy roads.
```
