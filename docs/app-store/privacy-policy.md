aigos ("the app," "we," "us") is a fuel-trip cost calculator for iPhone. We built
aigos to respect your privacy completely. This policy explains what the app does
and does not do with your information.

## The short version
aigos does not collect, store, or share your personal data. There are no accounts,
no cloud, no servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. Everything you
enter stays on your device.

## Information we collect
None. We have no servers and no backend. aigos does not ask you to create an account
or sign in. The trips, vehicles, prices, saved routes, and settings you create are
stored only on your iPhone (using Apple's on-device database) and are never
transmitted to us or anyone else.

## Location
aigos requests location access only when you tap the map button to set a starting
point, and only if you grant permission. Your location is used on your device to
center the map. We never store your location, never save it with your trips, never
link it to your identity, and never send it to us — because we have no servers to
send it to.

## Maps (Apple Maps)
When you use the optional map picker, aigos uses Apple's MapKit to show the map,
search for places, and estimate driving distance. Those requests are handled by
Apple under Apple's own Privacy Policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). aigos
sends no additional data of its own, and receives no identifying information back.
The rest of the app — calculating fuel cost, history, prices, and Talk to aigos —
works fully offline with no network use at all.

## Third-party services, analytics & advertising
aigos contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs of any kind.
We do not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) and we do not track you across apps
or websites.

## Data storage & deletion
All app data lives on your device. You can delete any trip or clear your entire
history at any time in Settings. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your
iPhone permanently.

## Children
aigos does not collect data from anyone, including children. It is safe for all ages
and contains no ads or tracking.

## Changes to this policy
If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the date above.

## Your rights (Philippines)
As we collect no personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct, or
delete on your behalf. This policy is offered in the spirit of the Philippine Data
Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).
